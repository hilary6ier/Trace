# Draft main-figure legends

## Figure 1. Technology-associated sequence structure persists across cellular contexts.

**A,** Concordance of five-mer technology-associated effects learned independently from exact-locus-disjoint HEK293T and HeLa maps. Each point is one motif observed in both training maps (n = 105); point area represents the smaller number of calls across the two cells. The dashed line denotes identity. Pearson r = 0.509 and Spearman rho = 0.430. **B,** Relationship between the frozen synthetic calibration contrast and human-map motif effects. Points are motifs observed in the indicated training map, with area proportional to the number of calls; solid lines show the frozen count-weighted calibration decomposition. Synthetic calibration explains a small component of human-map motif variation (weighted R2 = 0.049 in HEK293T and 0.081 in HeLa). **C,** Cross-cell discrimination by the full five-mer score and the calibration-residual score. Points show AUROC and horizontal lines show motif-bootstrap 95% CIs. Full-score AUROC was 0.734 for HEK293T-to-HeLa and 0.820 for HeLa-to-HEK293T transfer; corresponding residual AUROCs were 0.725 and 0.785. **D,** Robustness of cross-cell transfer after requiring held-out genes, restricting ELAP calls to higher/highest confidence, or excluding PUS7-like UNUAR motifs. Points show AUROC and horizontal lines show frozen 95% CIs. The dashed vertical line in C and D denotes AUROC = 0.5. TRACE-Ψ concerns measurement-evidence portability rather than biological truth.

## Figure 2. Evidence across distinct source technologies strongly stratifies independent-platform re-observation.

**A,** DRS re-observation across the seven observed BID/BACS/ELAP source-support patterns in HeLa. Filled squares mark assay membership, fractions give DRS-positive loci over the total source-positive loci, and points with horizontal lines show proportions with Wilson 95% CIs. **B,** Pooled DRS re-observation by the number of distinct supporting source chemistries: 14.4% (159/1,103) at breadth 1, 46.9% (84/179) at breadth 2, and 73.5% (36/49) at breadth 3. Error bars are Wilson 95% CIs. **C,** Motif-cluster-robust logistic estimate per additional source chemistry (OR 4.61, 95% CI 3.55–6.00). The dashed line denotes OR = 1. **D,** Observed difference in mean source breadth between DRS-positive and DRS-nonreported loci compared with a 20,000-draw null generated by permuting DRS labels within five-mer motifs. Grey points show a deterministic subsample of null draws, the open point and line show the null median and central 95% interval, and the red diamond shows the observed difference (0.444; frozen two-sided P = 0.0001). DRS support denotes independent-platform reporting, not ground truth.

## Figure 3. TRACE-Ψ resolves portability within breadth=1 single-source evidence.

**A,** HeLa leave-one-technology-out benchmark. Each cell gives held-assay AUROC for P0 breadth, P1 provenance, a context-only model, or TRACE-Ψ when BACS, BID, or ELAP was held as the target assay. Model selection and hyperparameter tuning used grouped internal development data only. **B,** Ranking of the independent HeLa DRS outcome within breadth=1 source evidence. Points and horizontal lines show AUROC with bootstrap 95% CIs: P0 breadth, 0.500; P1 provenance, 0.448 (0.406–0.487); context-only, 0.606 (0.557–0.655); and TRACE-Ψ, 0.644 (0.600–0.688). The dashed line denotes AUROC = 0.5. **C,** Paired bootstrap differences in breadth=1 AUROC for the predeclared benchmark contrasts. TRACE-Ψ improved on context-only by 0.038 (95% CI 0.008–0.068; bootstrap support for delta > 0, 0.994). Points show paired delta AUROC and horizontal lines show 95% CIs; the blue diamond marks the primary TRACE-Ψ versus context-only increment. TRACE-Ψ ranks cross-platform re-observation propensity and is not a model of biological truth.

## Figure 4. Frozen TRACE-Ψ transfers to an untouched A549 BID-to-DRS lockbox and improves practical validation yield.

The frozen TRACE-Ψ model was applied without retraining, recalibration, or feature changes to 761 locally single-U A549 BID loci; 42 loci (5.52%) were independently re-observed by DRS. **A,** Empirical ROC curve from individual frozen scores (AUROC 0.763, bootstrap 95% CI 0.677–0.846). The dashed line indicates random ranking. **B,** Empirical precision-recall curve (average precision 0.290); the dashed horizontal line is the lockbox prevalence. **C,** Tie-aware cumulative recovery of DRS-positive loci as an increasing fraction of loci is screened. The dashed diagonal indicates random ranking, and open points mark budgets of 50, 100, and 200 loci. **D,** Expected DRS-positive hits for exact validation budgets, treating loci tied at the cutoff as exchangeable. Frozen expectations were 18.3 hits at K = 50, 25.0 at K = 100, and 25.5 at K = 200; the dashed line is the prevalence-based random expectation. DRS non-report is not biological absence, and the TRACE-Ψ score is not P(true pseudouridylation).
